Welcome

Welcome to Rose Cottage

Welcome to Rose Cottage, a cozy homestay in Anachal, Munnar. Surrounded by misty hills and lush greenery, it offers a peaceful escape from city life.

Enjoy cool mountain air, warm hospitality, and a comfortable stay, perfect for couples, families, and nature lovers. Wake up to beautiful views, explore nearby tea gardens, and feel truly at home in the Munnar hills.
